Additional guidance for Claude Design
To make this website feel truly industry-defining, direct Claude to create an experience more like an Apple product launch combined with NVIDIA GTC, AWS re:Invent, and SpaceX. The site should use dark, cinematic visuals with glassmorphism, subtle neon blue and cyan lighting, animated telemetry, flowing data streams, holographic dashboards, interactive 3D digital twins, live topology maps, and immersive scroll-based storytelling. Visitors should feel they are looking at the operating system of the world's first autonomous data center rather than a traditional corporate website.
The experience should emphasize innovation, confidence, intelligence, and premium enterprise design throughout, making Freedom Data Center appear to define a new market category rather than compete in an existing one.
